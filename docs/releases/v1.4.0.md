## 2. Release Highlights & Deliverables

### 2.1 Authentic 2023–2024 WordPress Archive Import

- **Database Extraction**: Extracted authentic legacy data from the 2-year-old WordPress backup archive (`ba4a25b83b08-db.gz`):
  - **24 authentic historical events** across the 2023 and 2024 seasons (general meetings, field trips, annual plant sale, flower show, holiday luncheon, hands-on workshops).
  - **11 authentic historical venues** (Old School House Douglas, Saugatuck Woman's Club, Fenn Valley Vineyards, All Saints Episcopal Church, Wyncroft Winery, Saugatuck Center for the Arts, Spring Meadow Nursery, etc.).
  - **Heritage attribution**: Founding in **1928** and federation in **1947** with Michigan Garden Clubs District IV and National Garden Clubs Central Region.
- **Media Preservation**: Selected 37 high-resolution authentic photographs, uploaded to Cloudinary CDN (`sdgc-web`), and populated into 3 historical photo albums:
  - _46th Annual Garden Walk (2024)_
  - _Historic Garden Walks Archive (2018)_
  - _Youth Gardening & Community Workshops (2021)_
- **Reproducible Migration**: Captured in committed migration [`supabase/migrations/20261024120000_import_old_site_historical_archive.sql`](file:///j:/DevDrive/sdgc-web/supabase/migrations/20261024120000_import_old_site_historical_archive.sql).

### 2.2 Club Notice Board & Editorial Management

- **Public Notice Hub (`/notices` & `/notices/[slug]`)**:
  - Live announcements for meeting locations, weather alerts, volunteer calls, and seasonal tips.
  - Category filtering (General, Meeting, Urgent, Community, Workshop) with real-time search and pinned notice priority.
  - Dedicated notice detail pages with structured `NewsArticle` JSON-LD schema and canonical metadata.
- **Rich Markdown Formatting**:
  - Headings (H1–H3), bold text, bullet and numbered lists, blockquotes, code blocks, and sanitized links via `MarkdownContent`.
- **Admin Notice Studio (`/admin/notices`)**:
  - Create, edit, pin, unpublish, and delete notices with live side-by-side Markdown preview.

### 2.3 Strict AAL2 MFA Enforcement & Authentication Hardening

- **Middleware Step-Up Redirection**: Automatically intercepts authenticated sessions that have enrolled in TOTP MFA but haven't completed an AAL2 challenge, routing them directly to `/mfa-verify`.
- **Protected Dashboards**: Ensures protected member (`/profile`, `/dues`) and admin (`/admin/*`) surfaces cannot be accessed until AAL2 is verified.
- **Password-Confirmed Unenrollment**: Enforces current password verification before removing an enrolled MFA factor, preventing session hijackers from disabling MFA.

### 2.4 Photo Gallery & Garden Walk Lightbox

- **Accessible Lightbox Viewer**: High-resolution modal lightbox with `role="dialog"`, full keyboard navigation (`ArrowRight` for next, `ArrowLeft` for previous, `Escape` to close), and touch swipe support.
- **JnUITS-Inspired Album Archive**: Curated album view on `/gallery` with year badges, category tabs, photograph counters, and deep-linking to `/gallery/[slug]`.

### 2.5 Security Hardening & Zero CodeQL Alerts

- **CodeQL Alert Remediations**: Resolved all 5 active GitHub CodeQL security alerts:
  - High severity: Parser-level HTML sanitizer `sanitizeHtml` replacing naive regular expression tags.
  - Medium severity: WHATWG `URL` constructor protocol validation (`http:`, `https:`, `mailto:`, `tel:`) rejecting control characters and unsafe schemes.
  - Medium severity: URL attribute breakout prevention via `encodeURI` on markdown links and `encodeURIComponent` on album slugs.
- **Security Policy**: Established official [`SECURITY.md`](file:///j:/DevDrive/sdgc-web/SECURITY.md) guidelines and vulnerability disclosure SLA matrix.
- **Optimized CI Workflow**: Configured concurrency control (`cancel-in-progress`) and 30-minute timeouts in [`codeql.yml`](file:///j:/DevDrive/sdgc-web/.github/workflows/codeql.yml).

### 2.6 Dynamic Sitemap & SEO Completion

- **Comprehensive Sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`)**:
  - Static pages: `/`, `/about`, `/garden-walk`, `/events`, `/gallery`, `/notices`, `/membership`, `/contact`, `/join`, `/tickets-checkout`.
  - Dynamic events: All published events (`/event/[slug]`).
  - Dynamic notices: All published club notices (`/notices/[slug]`).
  - Dynamic photo albums: All public photo albums (`/gallery/[slug]`).
  - Dynamic CMS pages: All published custom pages.

### 2.7 Dependency Upgrade

- Upgraded Next.js from `16.4.1` to `16.4.2` with Turbopack compilation passing in 5.7s.
