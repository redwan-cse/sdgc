## Evidence limitations

Indexed extracts were readable for several public pages, and the Garden Walk page was readable directly through the web tool. Extracts have different crawl dates and may be stale. No authenticated WordPress session, database export, plugin list, payment-provider configuration, or private member content was supplied. This is a source-backed starting inventory, **not a completed parity certification**.

Confidence labels: **public evidence** means visible in a readable page or indexed extract; **inferred requirement** means necessary to replace the observed function; **unverified** means discovery is still required. Antigravity must update these labels with a date, source, and test as it discovers more.

**Owner confirmation, 2026-10-15:** the current site has a member login panel and a WordPress admin panel. The replacement must implement RBAC and member profile management. Their existence is confirmed; the exact private fields/screens/capabilities still require the P0 walkthrough. The new admin area is a required deliverable, not an optional enhancement.

## Registration field contract

Preserve username, first name, last name, email, password confirmation; address line 1/2, city, state, ZIP; home and cell phones; birth month/day; spouse first name; Master Gardener yes/no; gardening interests and work/volunteer experience. Birthday year is not observed and should not be collected. Preserve the eight project choices: Community Beautification, 6th Grade Workshop, New Member Reception, Club Website, Community Garden Recognition Awards, Garden Walk Fund Raiser, Community Charitable Contributions, Holiday Decoration Workshop. Check exact required/optional flags against live form/export during P0. [Registration source](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/becomeamember/).

The public snapshot offers both card and mailed check. The USD 50 rate is a dated 2026 setting, not a permanent hardcoded constant. [Membership source](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/membership/).
- Preserve public information about fundraising, beautification, garden awards, scholarships, and club programs. Descriptions of plant sales do not establish an online store, and scholarship information does not establish an online application system. [About source](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/aboutourclub/).
- The event archive exposes keyword/date navigation and three calendar views. Individual event pages expose venue and timing information. Inspect any export/subscription buttons in the browser before deciding their parity requirements. [Archive](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/events/), [month view](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/events/month/), [example detail](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/event/board-meeting-15/).

Do not infer it from button wording. [Garden Walk source](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/garden-walk/).
- The login screen offers username/email and password recovery. Preserve those paths and generic error messages without exposing email addresses through username lookup. [Login source](https://saugatuckdouglasgardenclub.com/login/).
- Public navigation includes About Our Club, Become A Member, Events, Gallery, Garden Walk, Contact Us, and Members with Login/Create New Profile. Facebook is linked at `https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100064728277239`. Contact Us may be an anchor rather than a standalone page; preserve deep links after inspecting the actual href.
